The Residual Price Fallacy

A prediction market shows a price produced by an order book, and an order book can be empty. On a thin book the number on screen is the residue of whoever quoted last, not an offer anyone will fill. A trader arrives, sizes up, finds no counterparty, and leaves — and nothing anywhere records that it happened. The market's page looks identical the next day.

Reactivity-Driven Enforcement

Somnia's reactivity precompile invokes a handler when the market emits, so validators — not us — drive the measurement. The handler reads the book at that instant, writes a block-pinned sample, and the registry evaluates a deterministic verdict from stored data alone. A breach forfeits the maker's bond on chain.
