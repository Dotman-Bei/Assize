The subscription filters on the pool's address with a wildcard topic, so every
log the pool emits triggers one callback. A block containing many pool events
therefore produces many samples that read the same book at the same instant.
They are real observations and none is fabricated, but they are redundant, and
the honest measure of how often the book was observed is the distinct-block
count above, not the sample count. Neither number is dropped here.
